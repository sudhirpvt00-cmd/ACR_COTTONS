# 📁 Shop Files Directory

Place your shop assets in the respective folders below. The application will read and use them for branding, catalog, and contact information:

- **`logo/`**: Place your brand logo image here (PNG, SVG, or JPG).
- **`products/`**: Place your product photos and saree images here.
- **`catalog/`**: Place your product list spreadsheet (CSV, Excel, or JSON).
- **`details/`**: Edit `shop_details.json` with your shop address, phone number, WhatsApp number, and about us text.
